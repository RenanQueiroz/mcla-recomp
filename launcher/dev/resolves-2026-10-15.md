# Resolves and texture reloads at 3x (2026-10-15)

Roadmap item 4: after `rexglue-clear-rectangle-draws.patch`, the GPU frame at
3x (2x MSAA) still spent 3.5 to 4 ms on resolves and texture loads. Windows,
RTX 5080, Ryzen 7 9800X3D, launcher settings.

## What was measured

The GPU timestamp dev patches (`gpu-timestamps.sdk.patch`,
`gpu-timestamps-vulkan.sdk.patch`, `texload-split-timestamps.sdk.patch`), with
temporary labels added for this (`TMPR4`, not kept):
- each resolve: destination address and size, format, the EDRAM range, MSAA,
  sample selection, and its steps timed separately (dumping the host render
  targets into the EDRAM buffer, the resolve shader, the clear);
- each texture load: its guest address, size and format.

One timedemo run a side, `MCLA_GT_EVERY=1`, frames inside the segment.

| 3x, per frame | D3D12, free roam | Vulkan, free roam | D3D12, race |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resolves | 54, 2.21 ms | 54, 1.95-2.2 ms | 65, 1.74 ms |
| of which the dump into the EDRAM buffer | 1.34 ms | 1.08 ms | 1.00 ms |
| of which the resolve shader | 0.77 ms | 0.72-1.0 ms | 0.63 ms |
| Texture loads | 57, 1.62 ms | 55, 1.85 ms | 64-79, 1.25 ms |

## What drives them

**Resolves.** Every frame the game resolves:
- the shadow map, four 640x640 4x MSAA depth quadrants (daylight only);
- the main scene's depth, each of its two tiles twice, to two addresses;
- the main scene's colour twice, as `8_8_8_8` from the 7e3 target and as
  `16_16_16_16_FLOAT` through its 64-bit view;
- the post-processing chain (640x360 down to 8x8 luminance) and reflection
  targets (256x256, 128x128).

Each resolve first dumps the host render targets over its range into the EDRAM
buffer, every sample at 3x, and then the resolve shader converts that into the
scaled resolve buffer. The dump is 60% of the resolve time. The SDK's
`direct_host_resolve` (on by default) only does the same dump today: its
comment says the shaders reading the host render target directly have yet to
come.

**Texture reloads.** A resolve marks its memory as written, so every texture
over it is decoded again from the scaled resolve buffer at its next use. The
screen-sized ones are reloaded every frame, some several times, because the
game reuses its memory:
- the 3840x2160 `8_8_8_8` texture at 0x06ACD000 three times a frame (0.32 ms
  on D3D12): that range holds the scene's colour, then its depth, then small
  targets;
- the scene's depth texture at 0x078B0000 twice (0.19 ms), once per scene tile;
- the 3840x3840 shadow map (0.27 ms) and the 64-bit scene colour (0.25 ms).

Each reload was two passes over 33 to 66 MB: the load shader decoding into a
scratch buffer, then a copy of that into the texture (`CopyTextureRegion`, or
on Vulkan the storage copy). The copy was half of the load time.

## The change

`patches/rexglue-texture-load-direct.patch` (setting `texture_load_direct`,
default on, both APIs): textures of resolved data with resolution scaling, one
level, 2D, one layer, in the formats of the 32bpb, 64bpb and both depth load
shaders, are decoded straight into the texture through an unsigned integer
view of the texel size. That is one pass instead of two. The new shaders are
the existing load shaders with image stores (`texture_load_direct_scaled.xesli`,
built with the xenia 0b2ffa3 shader sources the SDK's load shaders are built
from). On Vulkan it uses the storage view of `vulkan_texture_load_storage_copy`,
so it is NVIDIA only by default like that.

GPU timestamps, free roam at 3x, per frame:

| | D3D12, before | D3D12, after | Vulkan, before | Vulkan, after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Texture loads | 1.62 ms | 1.12 ms | 1.85 ms | 1.36 ms |
| 3840x2160 `8_8_8_8`, three loads | 320 us | 239 us | 319 us | 264 us |
| 3840x3840 shadow map | 267 us | 137 us | 269 us | 141 us |
| 3840x2160 depth, two loads | 192 us | 135 us | 209 us | 154 us |
| 3840x2160 `16_16_16_16_FLOAT` | 249 us | 111 us | 223 us | 131 us |

In the race, texture loads went from 1.25 to 0.94 ms a frame on D3D12 and from
1.50 to 1.24 ms on Vulkan (one run a side). Resolves are unchanged (1.7 ms).

Scripted timedemo (`launcher/dev/devrun.py`; updates of exactly 1/60 s, uncapped). `drive-spawn.pad` is fullscreen with five runs per configuration, `race.pad` windowed with three; runs alternate after a warm-up. Median frame time at 3x, with the 1% low in brackets:

| Segment | D3D12, off | D3D12, on | Vulkan, off | Vulkan, on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free roam, standing | 10.58 ms (73 FPS) | 10.15 ms, -4.1% (77 FPS) | 9.83 ms (87 FPS) | 9.40 ms, -4.4% (88 FPS) |
| Drive from the spawn point | 10.22 ms (73 FPS) | 9.69 ms, -5.2% (74 FPS) | 10.00 ms (81 FPS) | 9.41 ms, -5.9% (87 FPS) |
| Map zoom-out (loading) | 9.67 ms (25 FPS) | 8.12 ms, -16% (27 FPS) | 8.75 ms (32 FPS) | 7.86 ms, -10% (32 FPS) |
| Race | 10.41 ms (69 FPS) | 9.93 ms, -4.6% (72 FPS) | 11.86 ms (62 FPS) | 11.64 ms, -1.9% (62 FPS) |

- **The map zoom-out** varies 14 to 20% between runs, the race about 6% (`race-benchmark-2026-10-15.md`); free roam and the drive 2 to 3.5%.
- **Native resolution:** nothing changes, as nothing there is resolution-scaled (6.80 to 6.94 ms in every segment, on and off, both APIs).
- **D3D12 at 3x with a 60 FPS cap:** pacing unchanged, 96.6% of frames within 1 ms of the target on against 96.8% off.

## Checks

- **Screenshots** with the setting switched on and off within one run (the dev pad's `set`), 3 engine frames apart: standing in the city, in its pause menu, driving, in the map zoom-out and in the race (rain, opponents, HUD), on both APIs. Standing and paused, on against off differs no more than two shots with the same setting (mean 0.23 to 0.35 against 0.25 to 0.40 out of 255); the moving scenes show nothing out of place: shadows, reflections, the wet road, smoke, motion blur, the HUD and the minimap.
- **D3D12 debug layer** in that city run, with the setting switched on and off: no errors, the same few creation warnings as without the change. The `R32_UINT` and `R16G16B16A16_UINT` views of the typeless textures are accepted.
- **Vulkan validation** in that run: only `vkCmdEndQuery` 07007 and 01923. With synchronization validation, only the write after write between load dispatches into the scratch buffer that was there before; nothing on the images.
- **The attract movie** at 3x, D3D12 and Vulkan: every capture while it plays (221 and 222, every 150 ms) matches a frame of the decoded movie, with the largest difference 0.14, as in the earlier checks.

## Not done: options for later

| Option | Gain at 3x, estimated | Size and risk |
|---|---|---|
| Resolve straight from the host render targets, skipping the dump (what `direct_host_resolve` is meant to become) | most of the 1.0 to 1.3 ms of dumps a frame, about 10% | New resolve shaders for every variant (8 to 128 bpp, fast and full, scaled), reading MSAA host render targets in each host format, for DXBC and SPIR-V. Well over 800 lines, so left for now. |
| Skip a dump when the same range was dumped and nothing has drawn, cleared or transferred into those render targets since (the scene's depth is dumped twice in a row) | 0.12 to 0.18 ms | 150 to 250 lines in the shared render target cache; stale data if any write path is missed. |
| Dump only the samples a resolve reads (depth resolves take sample 0, but 4x and 2x dumps write every sample) | 0.1 ms at night, about 0.3 ms with daylight shadows | Changes the dump shaders the SDK assembles in code, for both APIs. |
| Reload only the rows a resolve wrote | about 0.2 ms | Changes how the texture cache invalidates textures; hard to verify with screenshots. |
| On Vulkan, storage copies or direct loads for textures with mips (a 1536x768 reflection texture with four levels still goes through `vkCmdCopyBufferToImage`) | about 0.13 ms on Vulkan | Per-level storage views. |

The remaining ownership transfers (about 0.3 ms) are the final 1x target
overlapping the scene's colour and the post-processing chain reusing tiles.
