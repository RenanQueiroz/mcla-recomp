# Direct3D 12 GPU hang (fork issues #44 and #56), 2026-11-04 and 11-05

**Symptom.** On Direct3D 12, `D3D12 device removed: HRESULT 0x887A0006 - DEVICE_HUNG` after 2 to 30 minutes (#56: RTX 5080; #44: RX 6900 XT, 9 of 11 sessions); Vulkan never. Every hang log ended at that line: the SDK enables DRED only with the debug layer.

**Repro.** v0.3.7, RTX 5080 (a driver frame cap of 240), Direct3D 12, resolution scale 3, `--fps 0`, HDR off, the title screen left alone for 10 minutes (it alternates the city view and the attract movie, about every 2.2 minutes). The hang came 4 to 30 seconds into a movie, in 20 of 35 runs of the day's builds (57%). A cap of 120 lowered the rate; the debug layer, GPU-based validation and NVIDIA Aftermath hid it; DRED hid it in part.

**Excluded by runs that still hung:** upload hoisting off (2 of 5 hung; an early 3-run sample without a hang had pointed at it, and #56 was told so and then corrected), threaded submission off (2 of 3), placed texture heaps off (3 of 3), `texture_load_direct` off (2 of 4), the descriptor index fix alone (1 of 4), and six options off at once (occlusion queries async, direct host resolve, batched resolve loads, the depth read state, Reflex, the MSAA line guard: 2 of 3).

**Where.** A local diagnostic build (not in this repository) enabled DRED without the debug layer and logged each command the deferred list replayed with a note (draw shaders, texture loads, uploads, barriers, resolves), matched to DRED's breadcrumbs at a device removal. In 5 of 5 hangs the GPU stopped in the same draw: a rectangle list of 3 vertices, vertex shader `609F70AC2F6FF4C4`, pixel shader `A6B9B6B0FA692C54`, right after the movie frame's texture load; DRED reported no page fault. The pixel shader (ucode disassembly from `--dump_shaders`) is the motion blur pass: about 16 texture fetches and a 4x4 matrix read at `c[69+a0]` to `c[72+a0]`, `a0` computed from a texture's alpha. It has no loop. In the host shader the index is `a0` (clamped to -256..255) plus the offset, into a constant buffer of 256 vectors bound as a root CBV.

**Why.** Root descriptors have no bounds check, so an `a0` outside -69..183 reads outside the float constants; Vulkan enables `robustBufferAccess`. With Aftermath's shader error reporting on, the System log got single `nvlddmkm` 153 events at the movie times (the first about 5 seconds into a movie) and the game went on: the driver trapped the error instead of hanging. Six PC defaults set back to the SDK's at once (render target trim, video memory pressure, anisotropic filtering 16x to 4x, hot pages, unclipped draws on the CPU, page state) also gave no hang in 9 runs; not explained (they change what the out-of-range read lands on, maybe), and not needed with the fix.

**Fix and check.** `patches/rexglue-dxbc-relative-constant-clamp.patch` clamps the index to 0..255. The same diagnostic build with it: 5 of 5 runs clean, no `nvlddmkm` event; without it: 1 of 2 control runs hung. Found on the way: `patches/rexglue-dxbc-bindless-descriptor-index.patch` (descriptor indices one too high; 149 of 600 host shaders read their last binding past the buffer).

**Elsewhere.** Xenia Canary has the same translator code for both. Other projects report Direct3D 12 device losts without a known cause (Xenia Canary issues; the GoldenEye and reblue recompilations), not shown to be this one. LARecomp's renderer (Plume) does not use this translator.

**Not done.** An AMD GPU, long play sessions, Linux, the PS5 (Vulkan).
