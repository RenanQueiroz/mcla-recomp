# Custom vinyls and the taillight artifact (issue #11), 2026-10-27

Two reports in one issue: (A) "the vinyls only work inside the vinyl editor, and disappear as soon as I leave the menu" (the same as in
Xenia; in Xenia Canary the setting `readback_resolve` fixes it, at a big cost in performance); (B) "in freeroam I get some strange motion blur
artifact near the taillights, disabling it doesn't solve it" (i5-8400 and GTX 1060 6 GB, settings unknown).

**State (2026-10-27, after the second round of runs, D3D12 and Vulkan on an RTX 5080 and, for the cost, the Ryzen's Radeon): (A) the bake of the cars' vinyls is
found and a rule restores them in the arcade race (default on, `mcla_vinyl_readback`); (B) the taillight blur was reproduced, it is not the game's motion
blur or depth of field, it is a Vulkan-only fault in how the speedometer's box is drawn, and an SDK option removes it at a cost (off by default,
`mcla_vulkan_depth_convert`). The first session had nothing run; its reading of the code is kept below.**

## What is known from reading

### A: vinyls

- The SDK has `readback_resolve` (`none`, `fast`, `some`, `full`; hot-reloadable) and the legacy `d3d12_readback_resolve` and
  `vulkan_readback_resolve`. It acts on every resolve (render target to texture memory): the destination is copied to a readback buffer and, for
  `full`, the command thread waits for the GPU and copies the result into guest memory at once; `fast` and `some` copy the previous frame's. At 3x
  the resolve first goes through a downscale compute pass to the 1x layout. So `full` costs a GPU wait per resolve, per frame.
- Xenia Canary's compatibility list (xenia-canary/game-compatibility #145, 545407F8) labels the game `gpu-readback` ("GPU-written data read
  on CPU (HDR adaptation, save images)") and `gpu-occlusion-query` (`occlusion_query = "fast"` "fixes unoccluded lens flares"). Vinyls are not
  named there, but the pattern is the one `readback_resolve` is for: a result the GPU makes (the vinyl layers drawn into a render target and
  resolved to a texture), which the game then reads with the CPU (to bake it into the car's texture), sees as stale memory when the emulation
  keeps it on the GPU, and so bakes an empty livery. The editor shows the layers because it draws them on the GPU every frame.
- The player's career save has no vinyls: `UserVinylData` in `mc4.sav` is a pool of 223 default entries
  (`00002fae2fae00000000ffffffff00000000ffff`), then 0xCD fill. A vinyl has to be made in the editor (garage, vinyl editor) to see the bug.
- Tools the tree has for finding the code that reads the texture: the dev pad (`launcher/dev/pad.py`, `src/dev_pad.cpp`: `screens` lists the UI
  manager's movies by name, `set`, `peek`, `dump`, `shot`), the render markers (`src/render_markers.cpp`: the game writes its frame and render
  phases into the command stream, `mcla_render_markers_all_phases`), the guest crash tracer (`src/crash_trace.cpp`: host address to guest
  function) and the sampling profiler (`mcla_profile`).

### B: taillights

- Defaults of the launcher (what a new player gets): Direct3D 12 ("Automatic"), native 720p, 30 FPS cap, 2x MSAA, frame generation off,
  motion blur on (the game's own setting, `mcla_motion_blur`), FSR sharpness 0.2.
- The night's light glows, coronas and beams (85 to 100 additive draws) are drawn after the tone map (phase 0x10000000,
  `frame-structure-2026-10-16.md`), in display space, together with 24 point draws without a target that are occlusion queries for the glows.
  The motion blur (`sub_8260E9A0`, post phase, 640x360) works from depth, the stencil vehicle ID and per-vehicle matrices, before the tone
  map; the pass runs in every 3D frame even with MOTION BLUR off (`rexglue-temporal-aa.patch`: it is the anti-aliasing point). So the setting
  does not remove everything that blurs, and the glows are not blurred at all.
- FSR 3.1 anti-aliasing runs over the scene texture before the motion blur with motion vectors made from the game's per-vehicle matrices;
  its trails are warned about in the launcher ("possible faint trails behind fast-moving things"). The glows are after it, so they
  can't be smeared by it. Frame generation interpolates the finished picture, glows included, without motion vectors for them.
- Occlusion queries are host queries (`occlusion_query_enable`, on by default; the fake count 1000 otherwise). With 2x MSAA and resolution
  scaling the sample counts are larger than on the console.

## What the patch does

`rexglue-resolve-readback-rules.patch` (`patches/README.md`): `readback_resolve_rules` reads back only the resolves a rule matches (address
range, length, destination pitch, height, format, source, render phase; mode `full` by default), whatever `readback_resolve` is, and
`resolve_log_frames` logs the resolves of the next N frames with those properties. Off (empty rules, `readback_resolve=none`) the resolve
code is the old one. The idea is the cheapest fix that needs no knowledge of the game's code: log the resolves in the vinyl editor and when
leaving it, find the one the CPU needs (by bisecting the rules), and ship that rule as a default for the title.

## How to find the rule (the runs)

1. Get a car that has vinyls into view. The garage is not a pause menu entry (see Results: the pause menu has Pause, Modes and Settings only); it is a place in the city (`src/warp_fix.cpp` handles the camera flight when the garage is picked on the map far from it). Story and arcade opponents carry vinyls of their own (issue #14: "even NPC cars are affected"), so the arcade race `Sunset and Vine` with 7 opponents is the test that needs no editor (`launcher/dev/scripts/arcade-race.pad`'s menu route, pictures of the opponents at the grid and while driving). Take `shot`s with `--readback_resolve=none` and `full`.
2. In the editor and after leaving it: `set mcla_render_markers_all_phases true`, `set resolve_log_frames 3`. The log lists the resolves with
   their render phase; the vinyl bake is the one that is not in the usual frame (no phase of the usual list, an odd size, or only there).
3. `set readback_resolve_rules len=...,pitch=...,height=...,fmt=...` with the properties of the candidates, one at a time, then leave and
   look at the car again. A rule that restores the vinyls and costs nothing in free roam is the fix: it goes in as the title's default (a
   `REXCVAR` default set at start-up in the game, like the other PC defaults).
4. If the destination is a fixed address the rule can use `addr`; if it moves, `pitch`, `height`, `fmt` and `phase` should still tell it apart.

## Results

### 2026-10-27 (first runs, D3D12 on an RTX 5080, scratch build of branch `fix/vinyls`)

- **The patch builds and runs.** `readback_resolve_rules` parses (an invalid rule is left out with a warning; `get` shows it), a rule that reads back every resolve of 1 MB or more ran for
  a second without a fault. The resolve log lists a frame's resolves with their render phase (`mcla_render_markers_all_phases`).
- **The log's window was wrong.** `resolve_log_frames N` counted the command processor's swap counter, which runs about 25 times per drawn frame in this title (33299 at guest frame 1094),
  so N = 2 logged a millisecond, up to the first resolves of the frame. Now it counts the title's frames (its frame marker) when there is one, and `resolve_log_new` logs each distinct
  resolve once, which is the tool for something that runs once.
- **What a city frame resolves** (dusk, standing, the part of the frame the window saw): phase 0x1: two 8_8_8_8 targets of 128x128 (`036F2000`, 64 KB) and 256x256 (`03702000`, 256 KB) every frame
  (the plate and, maybe, the car's livery composite: a guess); 0x2: the 256x256 reflection view (`05AC3000`, 16_16_16_16F, 512 KB); 0x4 and 0x8: the 512x256 environment map (`032E2000`, 1 MB) and its
  mip chain down to 64x32, which the car paint uses; 0x20, 0x40, 0x80 by day: the sun's cascades (a 1280x1280 depth atlas, source 4, 3 MB). The rest of the frame (phases from 0x400) was not in the window.
- **The pause menu has no garage entry.** Tabs: Pause (Continue, Save game), Modes (Arcade, Goal Attack, Xbox Live, Online Tournament, System Link, Race Editor), Settings (Options, Statistics, Cheat
  Codes, Music Playlists, Display, Performance); RB past Settings stays on Settings. The garage is a place in the city, so the vinyl editor needs a drive (or a position poke) there.
- **The career car has no vinyls** (the save's `UserVinylData` is the default pool), so free roam says nothing about the vinyls. What it does show about the global modes (the whole picture, mean difference
  from `none`, 0-255, noise 0.4): `full` 0.65, Vulkan `none` and `full` 0.86 and 0.85 (D3D12 against Vulkan), but **`some` 7.3 and `fast` 7.1: the picture turns purple** (mean blue +11): the global `fast`
  and `some` modes put stale data where the game's own textures are. Only `full`, or a rule with `mode=full`, is usable.
- Still to do: a car with vinyls in view (the arcade opponents), the resolve that carries the bake, the rule, the cost in free roam, the persistence checks.

### 2026-10-27, second round (D3D12 and Vulkan on the RTX 5080; the cost also on the Ryzen's Radeon iGPU, Vulkan device 1)

Scratch tools of the runs are not in the repository (they write pads and pictures under a scratch folder); what they did is described here.

**A: the vinyls.**
- **The test needs no editor.** In the arcade race *Sunset and Vine* (pause menu, Modes, Arcade, Ordered; 7 opponents, Easy, Clear, Noon: `launcher/dev/scripts/arcade-race.pad`'s menu route) the opponents carry vinyls. With `readback_resolve=none` the green opponent (a Nissan 240SX) is plain green at the grid, in the intro and while driving; with `readback_resolve=full` it is white and green with a 6, PIRELLI, WORK and HKS decals. (The first chain's V runs never ran: its setup stopped on "the patch has changed since it was applied" because the step asking for `-ResetSdk` passed it as an array element, which PowerShell binds to a positional parameter, not the switch; the pad itself worked the first time it was run.)
- **The log.** With a window of at most 3600 swap commands (the first build's limit), re-armed every 1.5 s through the race's loading, the log held 15,707 resolves of the city and the options screen (29 classes by length, pitch, height, format, source) and 115,275 of the loading, intro and start (36 classes). The race's new classes that are not drawn every frame: 1 MB 512x512 `8_8_8_8` (`0x050A0000`), 256x256 (`0x051A0000`), 128x128 (`0x051E0000`), 64x64 (`0x051F0000`), 32x32, 32x16, 32x8 and 32x4 (`0x051F4000` to `0x051F7000`), each 113 times, in the UI phase (`0x20000000`), guest frames 2523 to 2810: the mip chain of one 512x512 texture, drawn about every second guest frame while the 8 cars load. Also new: a 224x220 `8_8_8_8` (`0x06ACD000`, 1,556 times, post phase) and 1280x720 and 960x640 targets of the race's own frame.
- **The rule.** `len=1048576,pitch=512,height=512,fmt=6` (the base level alone) and `addr=0x050A0000-0x051F7FFF` (the whole chain) both restore the opponents' vinyls on D3D12; `len=200704,pitch=224,height=220,fmt=6` does not. The race loads in 5.3 s with or without the rule (`none` 5.3 to 5.6 s, `full` 5.5 to 5.8 s). The game's default rule is `len=1048576,pitch=512,height=512,fmt=6;addr=0x051A0000-0x051F7FFF`: the class finds the base level wherever the game puts it, the address adds the small levels (the CPU may read all of them; whether it does was not tested).
- **Cost in free roam:** the city's and the menus' 15,707 resolves have no 512x512 target, so the rule matches nothing there. A timedemo of the free-roam drive with the rule against none (D3D12 and Vulkan, 1x and 3x, display awake, exclusive lock) was prepared and not run (the lock was never free for it): that nothing is read back in free roam rests on the log.
- **Vulkan:** the same race with none, the chain by address and the game's rule: plain, vinyls, vinyls.
- **A second race in the same session:** quit the first (START, DOWN twice, A on QUIT RACE, A on YES of "Do you want to repair your vehicle before you QUIT?"; the pause menu of a race lists CONTINUE, NEXT RACE, QUIT RACE, RESTART RACE, ADD RACE TO FAVORITES, VIEW REPLAY, SAVE GAME), the city comes back, the same menu route starts the second race: cars with vinyls are drawn with them (D3D12, the rule set in the city by the pad; the opponents are picked at random, so they are not the same cars as in the first race).
- Not verified: see KNOWN-ISSUES (the editor, the story's cars, save and load).

**B: the blur over the plate and the rear lights.**
- **The first orbit was too fast.** The right stick's deflection is the chase camera's angle, not a turning speed: up to about 0.5 nothing (a dead zone), 0.6 about 30 degrees, 0.7 about 55, 0.8 about 70, 1.0 the side view, held, and the camera settles in half a second. The first batch's full deflection put the camera at the car's side in a second and never showed the rear again.
- **The reproduction.** A car creeping at a half throttle (13 to 17 mph), the camera swung from 0.5 to 0.95 and back in steps of 0.025 every 8 engine frames, a picture after each (about 7 a second); the time of day poked (`[0x8287E26C]+0x38CC` and `+0x38DC`: 17.5 h dusk, 23.5 h night; at night the lighting needs 12 s to follow the poke). At the rear 3/4 views (pictures 6 to 9 of the sweep) the plate's text is blurred in the part of it that is inside a rectangle at the right of the screen: its left edge is at the same x in every picture (the car moves, the rectangle does not), about 100 pixels to the left of the speedometer ring, and it covers the gauge box's height. Standing still (0 mph) the plate is sharp under the same box. D3D12 is sharp.
- **What was tried** (Vulkan, dusk, the same pad; plate crops at the same picture of the sweep): the game's depth of field off, the full-screen blur off, anti-aliasing off, occlusion queries off, `depth_float24_round`, `clear_rectangle_draws=false`, `depth_transfer_not_equal_test=false`, `direct_host_resolve=false`, `native_2x_msaa=false`, `readback_resolve=full`: the blur stays. The game's motion blur off (alone and with the depth of field off) leaves it fainter. `depth_float24_convert_in_pixel_shader=true` (also together with the rounding) and `render_target_path_vulkan=fsi` remove it, at dusk and at night. D3D12's default path has none; with the option on D3D12 the SDK fails to create some pipelines (186 errors a run) and the picture is the same.
- **The player's clip** (1280x720, 30 FPS): the HUD's speedometer is at the lower right and the car's rear is next to it in the 3/4 views; the gray-white smear sits on the bumper corner and the right taillight, left of the ring, as in the reproduction.
- **Cost of the fix** (timedemo of `drive-spawn.pad`, `--fixed-dt 60`, uncapped, Vulkan, display awake, exclusive lock, median frame): RTX 5080 at 3x 8.38 and 8.36 ms without, 10.23 and 10.09 ms with the option (+21%); at 1x 6.84 against 6.86 ms (CPU-bound); Radeon iGPU (Vulkan device 1) at 1x 35.05 and 35.13 ms without, 46.80 and 46.62 ms with (+33%; mean 28.7 to 21.2 FPS; 1% low 23.8 to 12.3 and 16.4).
- **Result:** the game's option `mcla_vulkan_depth_convert` (off by default) sets the SDK's option on Vulkan; see KNOWN-ISSUES.
