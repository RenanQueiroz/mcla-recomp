Follows `gpu-weak-2026-10-27.md` (same machine: the integrated Radeon of the Ryzen 7 9800X3D, "AMD Radeon(TM) Graphics", and the RTX 5080; the scripted
city run of `devrun.py`, GPU timestamps of `gpu-timestamps*.sdk.patch`, `--hdr=off`, 2x MSAA, windowed). Two changes, a diagnosis, and the tools. The
Radeon has "slow hours" in which everything takes 1.4 times as long: only runs next to each other (off, on, on, off) are compared.

What had and had not been run when this was written is listed at the end ("State").

## 1. Transfers the next draw rewrites (`rexglue-skip-overwritten-transfers.patch`)

Where the EDRAM ownership transfers of a frame go (Radeon, Direct3D 12, 1x, free roam, the frame log of `frame-log.sdk.patch`, joined with the draws):
1470 us in all. The biggest is the tone map: one rectangle over the whole 1280x720 `8_8_8_8` frame at EDRAM tile 720 (rectangle list, 3 auto-indexed
vertices, blending `One`/`Zero`/`Add`, all four components written, no depth or stencil, no kill, viewport and scissor 1280x720), whose binding copies the
overlapped tiles of the scene colour (2x MSAA `2_10_10_10_FLOAT`, tile 1024 on) into the new target first: 913 us. The post chain before it, about ten
rectangle-list draws into smaller targets in other formats at tiles 0 to 368, takes another 440 us.

The tone map's vertex shader (`VS 1FAB9787BCA32D7C`) fetches a texture (the adapted luminance, for the pixel shader), so the interpreter that runs vertex
shaders on the CPU for clear rectangles refused it. The patch makes the interpreter track what depends on a fetch (per temporary register, the previous
scalar result, and control: a predicate or address register set from such a value taints every export after it), and accepts a shader whose position and
vertex kill exports are clean.

How much of a rectangle is proven covered, by resolution scale (the tone map's rectangle, half-pixel offset applied; `GetOverwrittenRectangle`'s
arithmetic, edge cases of 1/1024 and 1/256 of a pixel without MSAA, 0.109375 with):

| | 1x | 2x | 3x |
|---|---|---|---|
| Columns / rows covered, no MSAA | 0 to 1280 / 0 to 720 | 1 to 1280 / 1 to 720 | 1 to 1280 / 1 to 720 |
| With MSAA | 1 to 1280 / 1 to 720 | 1 to 1280 / 1 to 720 | 1 to 1280 / 1 to 720 |

So at 1x the tone map is cut completely (its own pixels have no MSAA), and at 2x and 3x the first guest row and column are transferred as before (a strip).
The narrower post-chain rectangles are cut for the part inside them (the ownership change takes the rectangle as a cutout; what sticks out of it is
transferred).

Checking it cannot hide a missing transfer: `overwrite-poison.sdk.patch` (cvars `tmp_overwrite_poison`, `tmp_overwrite_log`) fills the region that is not
transferred with a junk pattern (`0x8040C0208040C020`) instead of leaving whatever was there, and logs `TMPOVW: CUT|kept ... line N` for each analysed draw
with the reason it was kept. A picture taken with the cut on and with it off must be identical; the pad of the harness (`set mcla_freeze true`, four shots
per scene: on, off, on, off) compares the pixels that are equal between the two shots of each state (a world that is not frozen moves a little) and wants
at least 90% of the picture comparable and no difference in them.

Planned runs (bit-exactness): Direct3D 12 and Vulkan on the Radeon at 1x, Direct3D 12 on the Radeon at 2x (free roam and the pause menu), Direct3D 12 and
Vulkan on the RTX 5080 at 3x. Timings: off, on, on, off of `skip_overwritten_transfers`, Radeon Direct3D 12 1x, Radeon Vulkan 1x, RTX 5080 Direct3D 12 3x.
Expected from the profile above: 3 to 4% of the Radeon's frame at 1x, 4.5 to 5.5% at 2x, 2.5 to 3.3% on the RTX 5080 at 3x.

## 2. Barriers before the depth resolves (`rexglue-d3d12-depth-resolve-read-state.patch`)

The resource barriers before the resolves took 0.905 ms of the frame at 1x on the Radeon (4.9 ms at 2x). All of it was in the six depth resolves of a frame (the two
1280x720 depth buffers, 204 and 72 us at 1x, and the four 640x640 4x MSAA sun cascades, 179, 159, 139 and 113 us); the barriers before the colour resolves took
about 1 us. `DEPTH_WRITE` to `NON_PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE` alone makes the driver decompress the depth buffer (its HTILE metadata); `DEPTH_READ |
NON_PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE` keeps it compressed. On Vulkan, on the same GPU, all barriers of a frame take 0.05 ms. The patch moves the depth render target of a
resolve (direct resolve, host depth store, dump) to the combined state; nothing but the resource state changes.

## 3. The 19 converted clear rectangles (not solved; measurements to take)

The game clears its render targets by drawing a rectangle; `rexglue-clear-rectangle-draws.patch` turns these into clears of the host render targets. In a
frame 19 of them take 2.03 ms (5.2%) on the Radeon at 1x and 0.49 ms on the RTX 5080 at 3x. From the frame logs of the Radeon (`clearctx.py`, `sizecorr.py`):
- the first clear of a render target right after a resolve costs 190 to 550 us (63 to 172 us on the RTX 5080 at 3x); the clears that follow on the same
  target cost 0.2 to 25 us;
- the cost is not proportional to the bytes cleared (R^2 about 0.04 between the cleared area and the time);
- a host render target is as tall as the EDRAM allows for its pitch (a 128x128 clear lands in a 320x4096x2 target), and depth-only 4x clears take about
  42 us while colour plus depth takes much more.

Two explanations, which measure differently: (A) no fast clear: the clear value passed to `ClearRenderTargetView` is not the one the resource was created
with (optimized clear value), so the driver takes the slow path, or the surface has metadata (DCC, FMASK) that has to be handled for the whole, oversized,
surface; (B) a bubble when the pass is switched (the barrier before the clear and the render pass change). The development layers:
- `clear-split-timestamps.sdk.patch`: brackets the barriers (`cbar`) and the call (`cclr`) of each clear separately (Direct3D 12 and Vulkan), labels each
  clear with the render target's name, the rectangle and the host texture size. Environment `MCLA_RT_HEIGHT_CAP=<rows>` caps the host render targets'
  height (diagnosis only, Direct3D 12): if the cost follows the height, the whole surface is being processed.
- `clear-by-drawing.sdk.patch`: environment `MCLA_CLEAR_BY_DRAWING=1` draws the clear rectangle with a pixel shader that outputs the clear colour instead of
  calling `ClearRenderTargetView` (Direct3D 12 draw-view formats only): if the first-clear cost goes away, it is the clear path's metadata handling.
- The D3D12 debug layer reports `CLEARRENDERTARGETVIEW_MISMATCHINGCLEARVALUE` when (A) holds; the batch `dbg1` collects its output.

Decision table: the barrier split big -> (B) or the depth decompression of section 2; the call big and the cap helps -> allocate render targets for the
height they are used at (a height class in the render target key; invasive, not done); the call big and clear-by-drawing helps -> ship that (a patch with
an off switch); mismatch messages -> create the render targets with the clear values the game uses.
