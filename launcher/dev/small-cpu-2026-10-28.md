# Poll wait and wake on demand by default with 5 or fewer logical processors? (2026-10-28)

**State: ON by default since v0.3.0** (decided after the chain below, whose verdict for 4 cores without SMT was FAIL: see Results). The chain was run on the build of branch `cpu/small-cpu-defaults` (bbf43c6, the switch
still off, the variants set on the command line); the numbers are in "Results". The default was then flipped (`mcla_small_cpu_defaults`, `src/mcla_app.cpp`) in the integration
branch; that build was not run again for this.

## The switch

`mcla_small_cpu_defaults` (Windows, default ON since v0.3.0, `src/mcla_app.cpp`, `ApplyCpuDefaults`). On, a process that may run on 5 or fewer logical processors gets
`mcla_pump_wake_on_demand` and `mcla_poll_wait` on by default, next to the `timer_queue_hr_wait` it already gets there. A value from the command line, the environment or
`mcla.toml` wins, as for the other automatic values. The `CPU:` line says what was chosen:
`CPU: 4 logical processors: timer queue high-resolution wait on, pump wake on demand on, poll wait on (automatic; 5 or fewer, with mcla_small_cpu_defaults)`. With 6 or more logical
processors the switch changes nothing (the line is the same with it on). The cvar's default was `false` on the branch and is `true` in v0.3.0 (one word in `mcla_app.cpp`); `--mcla_small_cpu_defaults=false` is the old rule.
Linux and the PS5 do not have it (the other CPU defaults are Windows only too).

## Evidence so far (from the cpu3 and hand-off chains, 2026-10-28; RTX 5080, D3D12, native resolution, a Ryzen 7 9800X3D limited by affinity)

- At affinity 0xF (4 logical processors = 2 physical cores with SMT; logical processors 2k and 2k+1 are one core on this PC), uncapped, the update thread spent 47% of a frame waiting for
  a command ring slot (one traced run): the frame was the pump's 7 ms Sleep rhythm (6.96 ms).
- `mcla_pump_wake_on_demand` at 4 logical: uncapped frame 7.09 to 4.90 ms and 6.93 to 4.58 ms (swaps/s +39% and +51%), 2 runs a side; no benefit at a 30 FPS cap. Audio in those 2 runs:
  0 steady underruns, 7 and 6 late wake-ups (8 ms or more), longest 9.6 and 9.9 ms. The off run that opened its batch had 574 steady underruns on its own.
- `mcla_poll_wait` at 4 logical, uncapped: frame -4.4% (+-0.5), p99 -9.0%, process CPU -26% (+-0.9), Main thread -37% (toggle inside a run, 3 windows); A/B 2 runs a side CPU -28% and -30%.
  At caps (30, 60, 120) and at 8 and 16 logical it was noise.
- Not measured: both together; 0x55 (4 physical cores without SMT siblings) and 0x155 (5 cores); 5 runs a side; the audio over such a set; 6 logical with the switch (the proof that it
  changes nothing there).

## The chain (`SP\smallcpu\chain.ps1`, written, not run)

Variants `off` (`--mcla_small_cpu_defaults=false`) and `on` (`=true`) of one build, A-B-B-A order, n=5 a side after a warm-up, on 0x55 (4 cores), 0xF (2 cores with SMT), 0x155 (5 cores),
and one pair at 0x3F (3 cores with SMT = 6 logical). The route: boot, then free roam uncapped, a 60 FPS cap, a 30 FPS cap. A mask passes when, for the `on` runs against the `off` runs:
the frame median is at most +1.0% and the p99 at most +3.0% in every segment, the caps hold within 1%, the audio has no steady underrun and no watchdog trip in the run (and none at
shutdown), the swap-interval spread is not worse, the game clock is 1.000 +- 0.01, and either the uncapped frame is 3% shorter or the process uses 10% less CPU per swap, by more than the
runs' own spread. The report prints PASS, LOOK or FAIL per mask and the noise it measured.

## Results (chain `smallcpu`, 2026-10-28; exe = cpu/small-cpu-defaults at bbf43c6, sha256 ce06f2437dc09289...)

RTX 5080, Direct3D 12, native resolution, windowed, display awake, a Ryzen 7 9800X3D (8 cores, 16 logical, SMT) with the process limited by affinity; `off` and `on` as
`--mcla_small_cpu_defaults=false|true`; A-B-B-A, a warm-up first. Rule: frame at most +1.0% and p99 at most +3.0% in every segment, caps within 1%, no steady underrun or watchdog
trip, intervals not worse, clock 1.000 +- 0.01, and a gain of 3% in the uncapped frame or 10% in CPU per swap beyond the runs' spread. Frame and p99 are the change of the mean, on against off.

- **0x55 (4 cores without SMT)**, per run (free-roam frame median, read from the runs' frames.csv; the on runs are 2, 3, 6, 7 and 10, the off runs 1, 4, 5, 8 and 9):
  on 4.151 / 4.150 / 6.835 / 6.717 / 4.151 ms, off 6.855 to 6.943 ms. Runs 2, 3 and 10 are about 40% shorter. Runs 6 and 7 had one watchdog trip each (2 and 5 silent audio frames,
  at 20:43:39 and 20:45:01, about 21 and 23 s into the run: the title screen and the map zoom, before the free-roam part); after the trip the pump thread goes back to the game's
  Sleep for 60 s, which covered their measured segments, so they read like the old default (free-roam p99 7.8 and 7.7 ms, off 8.2 to 19.8). The p99 of the chain's rule is run 10:
  free roam p99 21.7 ms and driving 29.5 ms with a frame median of 4.15 ms, 77 late audio wake-ups (8 ms or more; the other 9 runs 4 to 44), no silent frame, no trip: a tail of long frames
  with the option working. Its cause is not known (not looked into). Runs with it OFF had silent frames too (3 in run 4, 1 in run 8; there is no watchdog without the option), so the
  dropouts at the start of a run are not only the option's. The return of the wake after the 60 s pause was not seen (the "back on" counter is 0 in both runs: the pause ended
  after the pad did).
- **Audio** (counted, not listened to): 0xF no steady underrun in 20 runs, late wake-ups of the audio worker (8 ms or more) 6.6 a run on, 23.7 off; 0x155 none, 1.4 on, 0.2 off;
  0x3F none, 1.5 on, 1.0 off. No `ran dry` line at or after the end of the pad in 44 of 44 runs (the shutdown fix).
- **Caps:** at 60 and 30 FPS on every mask the frame and the p99 are within 0.3%, the caps held (within 1%), the process's CPU per swap -2.3 to +2.6% (allowed up to +15.6 to +40.9%),
  the interval spread not worse; at 0x55 no early wake of the pump under a cap (the other masks' counts under a cap were not read).
- **Early wakes of the pump** uncapped with it on: 197 to 240 a second (0xF, 0x155; 139 at 0x55 where two runs paused), 0 off and at the caps.
- **Not covered:** one machine (the affinity job keeps the clocks, the IPC and the 96 MB of L3, so these are not a slow CPU's 4 or 5 logical processors); Direct3D 12 only; the audio by ear;
  Linux and the PS5; the pictures (timing only; the game clock is the only check of the simulation); 3 or fewer logical processors; the build with the flipped default was not run.
