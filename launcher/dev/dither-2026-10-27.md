# The lattice of dots on opaque geometry (fork issue #8), 2026-10-27

What the owner of issue #8 sent (an RTX 2060, a 1600x900 window, D3D12, v0.1.2/v0.1.3: "white texture/block issues", "I tried all settings"): a story cutscene at sunset in which the columns of the bus shelter behind three men are a fine grid of bright and dark dots, the dots reaching the edge of a head in front of a column; and a night picture of the free-roam spawn, a clothes shop's window with a lamp pole and a bush in front of it, dotted exactly inside the window's rectangle. The issue was closed as "an emulation artefact"; `KNOWN-ISSUES.md` had called "the same dithered pattern on thin geometry" normal. It is a bug of the alpha to mask emulation, fixed by `patches/rexglue-alpha-to-mask-tolerance.patch`. The pictures are not in the repository.

## How it was found

- **The period.** A Fourier transform of the pole in the owner's cutscene picture has its peaks at 0.4 cycles per pixel along x and along y (and not along the diagonal): 2.5 pixels, which is 2 pixels of the 1280x720 picture shown at 1600/1280 = 1.25. So the game ran at 1x, and the pattern is one pixel in every 2x2 quad of the guest's grid: what alpha to mask's dither offsets (the four 2-bit fields of `RB_COLORCONTROL` >> 24) make.
- **The place.** The free-roam spawn at dusk (`boot-to-roam.pad`, then `poke [0x8287E26C]+0x38CC 0x418C0000` and `+0x38DC`, 17.5 hours, and a few seconds for the light to settle) is the owner's night picture's place: the same shop windows, from the car's rear. With the camera as it is, the dark lamp pole in front of the window has the dots in the rows of the window and none below it, on D3D12 and Vulkan, with a night (`0x41BC0000`, 12 seconds) as with a dusk. They are in the pictures made for the vinyl work too (an RTX 5080, 1x, the default anti-aliasing), so they are not specific to the owner's machine.
- **The head.** The dark dots on the lit skin at the ear of the man on the right in the owner's cutscene picture (where the dark column is behind his head) are most likely the same thing (not checked: the draw was not identified): if a character's skin is drawn with alpha to mask too, the pixel that loses its second sample shows what is behind, dark there and invisible where the skin itself is dark.
- **The pixel.** `pole-dots.py` counts the pixels of the pole that are brighter than their column by parity of x and y: D3D12 61 at (x odd, y even) against 9 to 18 at each of the other three (the noise of the window behind), Vulkan 52 at (x even, y odd) against 7 to 17. The two translators number a pixel's place in its quad differently (D3D12 `2 * (x & 1) + (y & 1)`, Vulkan `(x & 1) + 2 * (y & 1)`), and both numbers are 2: the offset field 2 of the game's 3, 1, 0, 2 is 0, which makes the threshold of sample 1 of 2x MSAA 1 - 0 / 8 = 1.0 (sample 0's is 0.5): that pixel needs an alpha of 1.0 exactly.
- **The draws.** `fl-a2m.py` lists the draws of a frame log (`frame-log.sdk.patch`: `cc=` is `RB_COLORCONTROL`) with alpha to mask. The city frames of the GPU time work (`C:\mcv\igpu\gt`, at day) and the new one at dusk have 703 and 704 of about 3,700 draws, all `cc=8700001C` (alpha test greater than 0, alpha to mask, offsets 3, 1, 0, 2), all in the 2x MSAA scene, none anywhere else: 295 billboards of the distant trees (`EEFF113E5FD82321`: alpha is the impostor's texture alpha times a vertex alpha times `c26.w`), 141 + 41 draws of the sun-shadow collector (`7682F8C2B4A4A628` and `8E8CDF9E9BA9CF0A`: a 4-tap compare in the 640x640x4 cascade atlas, written as the colour with the vertex's alpha as alpha), 98 world draws (`ED5E3B21E269C02F`, alpha `c26.w`, a constant that is 1 on every draw whose constants the log prints), foliage (`6EBF788B1C5456FC`, `1C6267A307253175`, `4C6E93D24108070B` and more). Which of them draws the lamp pole was not identified; the dots are on every draw whose alpha is a hair under 1.0.
- **The switches** (`alpha-to-mask-debug.sdk.patch`, the development switch `tmp_a2m`, hot reload: 1 alpha to mask off, 2 the offsets forced to 2, 2, 2, 2) at the dusk spawn of free roam, one run, D3D12, 1x, 8 engine frames after each switch: default 61 dots at (x odd, y even); `tmp_a2m 1` 8 (the noise); `tmp_a2m 2` 8; `tmp_a2m 0` again 58. With the anti-aliasing off (`mcla_anti_aliasing off`): none, with or without alpha to mask (a different set of draws, perhaps without alpha to mask: not checked). Single tile off 57, on 61; shadows off 41 (the dots stay, so it is not the shadows); anti-aliasing back on 61. At night the same lattice is on the same pole (the lattice energy of `dmetric`: 0.3% of the tiles over the threshold with alpha to mask on, 0.0% with it off). So the dots are alpha to mask's, from the pixel with threshold 1.0, and nothing else's.
- **The size of the error** (a development build of the same change as a restart-only option, `tmp_a2m_eps`, thresholds lowered by eps / 4096): eps 2 (0.0005) 8 dots, eps 8 (0.00195) 5, eps 24 8, eps 128 7: the alpha is under 1.0 by less than 0.0005, a rounding error, not a fraction. `alpha_to_mask_tolerance` is 0.00195 (eps 8), half a step of an 8-bit alpha, which is what the console's hardware most likely compares (not known). Xenia Canary has the same translator code and a `use_fuzzy_alpha_epsilon` (alpha test, 1e-3, off by default) "to prevent flickering on NVIDIA graphics cards".
- **The console.** The story's first cutscene, from Xbox 360 footage (a YouTube video "All Cutscenes" at 1080p of about 1 Mbps; "Full cinematics" at 4K, 12 Mbps, an upscale of 720p): the bus shelter's posts are solid dark against the bright sign and the building behind them (the 4K frame at about 1:53), with no dots where the owner's picture has them. Compression could hide dots; a lattice of one pixel in four at half contrast, upscaled 3x, would not be hidden at 12 Mbps in the hard edge of a post across a bright sign. The title screen's palm scene (the owner's place too) is a different shot in 4K: nothing in it to compare.
- **Xenia.** The Xenia and Xenia Canary compatibility reports of Midnight Club: Los Angeles (xenia-canary game-compatibility #145, xenia-project #426) don't mention dots or dithering (they mention flicker at 2x, the vinyls and the traffic lights' reflections).

## Not done

- The title screen's palm scene (which the owner says shows it almost always, more at 1x): the title shows one of its scenes per start (a bridge downtown in the run here, the hills with a palm on the console's footage) and it was not reached. A matrix of switches on the title screen was prepared (`rov`, `native_2x_msaa`, the float24 options, direct resolves, clear rectangles, single tile, impostors, foliage shadows, 2x and 3x, Vulkan) and not run: the dusk spawn answered the question first.
- AMD and Intel GPUs, the PS5 and Linux: nothing was run on them.
- Whether the console's hardware rounds alpha: not known. The D3D12 and Vulkan numbering of a quad's pixels differ; one of the two dithers on the wrong pixels in relation to the console, which alpha to mask with an alpha of a fraction would show. Not checked.
- A frame log of the cutscene itself.

## Tools

`pole-dots.py`, `fl-a2m.py` (both here, plain Python with Pillow and NumPy), `alpha-to-mask-debug.sdk.patch` (the layer after `frame-log` for `gt-build.ps1 -Layer gpu-timestamps,gpu-timestamps-vulkan,texload-split-timestamps,resolve-split-timestamps,frame-log,alpha-to-mask-debug`, on an SDK with the fix patch). A frame log of a scene: the run's `--tmp_frame_log_dir=<dir> --mcla_render_markers_all_phases=true` and the pad's `set tmp_frame_log <label>`.
